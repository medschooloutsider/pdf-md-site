## Page 1 - Sampling Design

Title: Research Methods Field Notes

Subtitle: Coastal water quality pilot, synthetic teaching packet

Key question: Can a simple field protocol detect a short-lived turbidity spike after rainfall?

Protocol:

- collect three surface samples at each site
- record air temperature, water temperature, and turbidity
- rinse the probe before every site change
- flag readings that differ from the site median by more than 15 percent
