# Review Notes

This demo is about reviewability.

## What to inspect

- The source PDF has normal text, a table-like region, an equation sentence, and a raster note block.
- The Markdown output should be readable enough to edit, reorganize, and save in a notes workflow.
- The audit JSON exists so the run is inspectable beyond the final `.md` file.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim perfect OCR.
- Do not claim every table will preserve exactly.
- Do not claim academic or medical correctness.
- Do not claim that PDF-MD removes the need to check the source.

## Intended buyer-facing takeaway

PDF-MD can turn a messy academic-style source into a Markdown draft while keeping the review step visible. The user still owns the final check.
